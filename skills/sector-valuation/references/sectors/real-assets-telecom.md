# Real Assets, Utilities & Telecom Playbooks

REITs · Regulated utilities · Telecom & cable · Towers & fiber · Conglomerates & holding companies

---

## REITs

**Value equation:** NAV = NOI ÷ market cap rate (by property type and quality) − debt − preferred + other assets; earnings power = AFFO.

**KPIs:** FFO and AFFO per share (AFFO = FFO − recurring capex − straight-line rent adjustments) · same-store NOI growth · occupancy · leasing spreads · lease maturity profile · loan-to-value and debt maturities · implied cap rate.

**If undisclosed:** market cap rates from broker surveys (CBRE, Green Street) by sector; implied cap rate = (forward NOI) ÷ (EV); recurring capex from peers when the REIT doesn't split it.

**Benchmarks:** LTV <35–45% conservative; occupancy >90% (sector-dependent).

**Valuation:** P/NAV (premium/discount justified by growth and capital access); P/AFFO vs peers and growth; dividend yield vs coverage.

**Traps:** GAAP EPS (depreciation of appreciating assets) — never use; FFO that ignores recurring capex; office/retail cap rates that haven't marked to transactions.

## Regulated utilities

**Value equation:** Earnings = rate base × equity ratio × allowed ROE × earned-to-allowed ratio; growth = rate base CAGR (capex plan) funded by debt + equity.

**KPIs:** rate base growth (5-yr capex plan) · allowed ROE and equity layer by jurisdiction · earned vs allowed ROE (regulatory lag) · regulatory-jurisdiction quality · equity issuance needs · FFO/debt (credit metric) · load growth (data centers) and large-load tariffs · wildfire/storm liability.

**If undisclosed:** allowed ROEs from state PUC rate orders (RRA data); rate base by jurisdiction from investor decks.

**Benchmarks:** allowed ROEs ~9.5–10.5% (US, recent years — refresh); EPS growth 5–8% typical for capex-heavy plans; FFO/debt >14–15% for solid ratings.

**Valuation:** P/E vs utility group, premium/discount for growth and jurisdiction; DDM; rate-base multiple (EV/rate base).

**Traps:** equity dilution needed to fund capex; regulatory lag in inflationary periods; wildfire liability (western utilities); data-center load forecasts that don't materialize.

## Telecom & cable

**Value equation:** Subscribers × ARPU × (1 − churn) − network opex − capex; mature industry → FCF per share is king.

**KPIs:** broadband/wireless net adds · ARPU · churn (monthly) · EBITDA margin · capex intensity · FCF per share · leverage (net debt/EBITDA) · fixed-wireless and fiber overbuild competition.

**Benchmarks:** postpaid phone churn ~0.8–1.0%/month leaders; capex/sales 12–20%; leverage 2.5–3.5x.

**Valuation:** EV/EBITDA, FCF yield, DCF with explicit capex cycle; spectrum value in SOTP.

**Traps:** spectrum amortization treated inconsistently; promotional handset subsidies; secular broadband share loss to fiber/FWA.

## Towers & fiber infrastructure

**Value equation:** Sites × tenants per site × rent with escalators; incremental tenants at near-100% margin.

**KPIs:** organic tenant billings growth · churn (carrier consolidation) · AFFO per share · leverage · international FX/credit exposure.

**Valuation:** P/AFFO, EV/EBITDA, DCF on escalator-driven cash flows.

**Traps:** carrier consolidation churn; rate sensitivity of high-multiple long-duration assets.

---

## Conglomerates & holding companies

**Value equation:** Σ parts (each with its own playbook) + net cash − holdco costs − holdco discount.

**KPIs:** per-segment economics · capital-allocation track record (ROIC on acquisitions, buybacks below intrinsic value) · insurance float (if any) · look-through earnings.

**Valuation:** SOTP (`valuation.py sotp`) with 0–25% holdco discount based on capital-allocation quality, tax leakage and control; for owner-operator holdcos, book value growth + look-through earnings.

**Traps:** applying one multiple to unlike parts; ignoring tax on unrealized gains in investment holdings; key-person risk.
