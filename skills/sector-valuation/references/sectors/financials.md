# Financials Playbooks

Banks · P&C insurance · Life insurance · Asset & wealth managers · Brokers & trading platforms · Payments & card networks · Stablecoin issuers & crypto platforms

For financials, debt is raw material: use equity-based valuation (P/B, P/E, DDM), not EV/EBITDA.

---

## Banks

**Value equation:** P/TBV = (ROTCE − g) / (COE − g), with ROTCE = (NII + fees − opex − normalized credit cost) × (1 − t) / TCE.

**KPIs:** NIM and its rate sensitivity · deposit beta and mix (non-interest-bearing %) · loan growth · efficiency ratio · NCOs and reserve coverage (ACL/loans) · CET1 vs requirement (incl. stress capital buffer) · CRE/office exposure · AOCI marks on securities · ROTCE.

**If undisclosed:** NII sensitivity is disclosed in the 10-K market-risk section (±100bp tables) — use it; normalized credit cost from the bank's own through-cycle average NCO by loan category (FDIC QBP for industry averages); deposit beta from cost-of-deposits change ÷ Fed funds change over the last cycle.

**Benchmarks:** efficiency ratio <55% strong, >65% weak; CET1 buffer 1–2pts over requirement typical; sustainable ROTCE 12–18% for good franchises.

**Valuation:** justified P/TBV on **normalized** ROTCE (normal credit costs, forward-curve NIM); P/E on normalized EPS; excess capital valued at 1x and added.

**Traps:** peak NIM capitalized; reserve releases in core EPS (R3 — normalize); held-to-maturity losses invisible in TBV; deposit flight risk (uninsured %); regulatory capital rule changes.

## P&C insurance

**Value equation:** Underwriting profit (premium × (1 − combined ratio)) + investment income on float; P/B justified by normalized ROE.

**KPIs:** combined ratio ex-cat and ex-prior-year development · cat load vs budget · reserve development (favorable/adverse) · premium growth (rate vs exposure) · investment yield and new-money yield · float growth · reserve-to-premium · RBC/solvency.

**If undisclosed:** normalized cat load = 10-yr average cat losses as % of earned premium; reserve adequacy from Schedule P triangles (statutory filings) or 10-K loss development tables.

**Benchmarks:** best underwriters run combined ratios in the low 90s through the cycle; persistent favorable reserve releases eventually run out.

**Valuation:** P/B vs normalized ROE; P/E on cat-normalized earnings; for float-rich insurers, value float at cost-of-float advantage.

**Traps:** cat-light years capitalized; reserve releases as "earnings"; social inflation in casualty lines; reinsurance cost spikes.

## Life insurance & annuities

**Value equation:** In-force value (PV of future profits on existing book) + new-business value; spread on general account assets.

**KPIs:** embedded/appraisal value · new-business margin · spread · statutory capital & free cash flow to holdco · LDTI (US GAAP) effects · private-credit / alternative asset exposure.

**Valuation:** P/B (ex-AOCI) vs ROE; price/embedded value; FCF to holdco yield.

**Traps:** asset risk in private credit; reinsurance to affiliated offshore entities; assumption reviews causing charges (R1 not R0 if annual).

## Asset & wealth managers

**Value equation:** AUM × fee rate × margin; AUM growth = organic flows + market.

**KPIs:** organic flow rate (% of beginning AUM) · fee rate trend (mix to passive/alts) · performance fees (R3) · operating margin · market beta of AUM.

**Valuation:** P/E on core (ex-performance-fee) earnings; EV/EBITDA vs peers; for alt managers, separate fee-related earnings (higher multiple) from performance revenue (lower multiple).

**Traps:** market-driven AUM growth mistaken for skill; fee compression; performance fees capitalized.

## Brokers & retail trading platforms

**Value equation:** Funded customers × assets per customer × monetization (transaction revenue + net interest on cash/margin + subscriptions/other).

**KPIs:** funded customers · total platform assets · net deposits (annualized % of assets) · ARPU · revenue mix (options/equities/crypto transaction vs NII vs subscription) · cash sweep balances and spread · margin book · rate sensitivity per 25bp.

**If undisclosed:** rate sensitivity = interest-earning cash × 25bp × retention share; transaction revenue per notional traded from monthly metrics releases.

**Valuation:** P/E on **mid-cycle** earnings (normalize crypto/options volumes — R3 — and NII at the forward rate curve); platform-asset value (EV as % of assets) vs peers; DCF on net-deposit growth.

**Traps:** capitalizing peak crypto volumes; NII falling with rate cuts; regulatory risk to payment-for-order-flow; one-time IPO SBC catch-up (genuine R0).

## Payments & card networks

**Value equation:** TPV/purchase volume × net yield (take rate after rebates/incentives) − processing cost.

**KPIs:** volume growth (cross-border share — highest yield) · net revenue yield · client incentives % gross revenue · transaction margin (processors) · branded vs unbranded mix.

**Valuation:** P/E / DCF; networks earn premium multiples for toll-road economics; processors on EV/EBITDA with take-rate compression assumptions.

**Traps:** incentive growth outpacing gross revenue; litigation (interchange) as R1; real-time-payment rails / stablecoins as substitution threat.

---

## Stablecoin issuers & crypto platforms

**Value equation (issuer):** Average circulating stablecoins × reserve yield − distribution/partner payments − opex. Highly sensitive to short rates and circulation.

**KPIs:** circulation (and share vs competitors) · reserve yield (T-bill rate) · share of reserve income paid to distribution partners (read the partner agreement) · other revenue (transaction, subscription) · regulatory status.

**If undisclosed:** reserve income ≈ avg circulation × 3-month T-bill yield; sensitivity per 25bp and per $10B of circulation.

**Valuation:** DCF across explicit rate scenarios (forward curve, low-rate) × circulation scenarios; EV / (revenue less distribution costs).

**Traps:** valuing at peak rates; distribution economics that cap margin; circulation tied to crypto cycle (R3); regulatory change on yield-sharing.
